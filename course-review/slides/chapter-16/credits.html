Changes: original 1991–2024 selection, three-panel SVG visualization, separate unweighted OLS inflation-on-unemployment fits and Pearson correlations. Each country has all 34 annual pairs, using common axes. Unemployment is a modeled ILO estimate; national CPI baskets differ. Fits describe within-country association across years, not a causal effect or a permanent policy trade-off. No endorsement by the World Bank, IMF or ILO is implied.
