Chapter 17: Sources

Macroeconomics: Events and Ideas · ECON_OX 112

Explanations, numerical examples and schematic model diagrams were independently written and drawn for this course. Model diagrams have no empirical scale. The business-cycle graph uses fictional years and GDP indexes to illustrate successful stabilization around unchanged potential output; it is not an empirical estimate. Historical sources support the dated facts on the relevant slides; their articles and media are linked rather than reproduced.
