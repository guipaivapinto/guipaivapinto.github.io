OECD Chart Data

OECD (2026), Financial market statistics: long-term interest rates, OECD Data Explorer (accessed November 2, 2026).

Original chart from 64 annual observations for the United States, United Kingdom, Germany and Japan, 2010–2025. Approximately 10-year nominal government yields, in each country’s currency, annual averages. National definitions differ: UK par yields, German residual maturities over 9–10 years, and a Japanese definition change in November 2014. Values are selected and pivoted without alteration or interpolation. Selected values.
