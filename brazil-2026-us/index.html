How to read these results

  • A reporting centre is a Brazilian consular jurisdiction. It can include several voting locations. These are consular-centre results, not a classification of voters’ residences. New York’s jurisdiction also includes Bermuda.
  • A polling location here means the combination of municipality, zone and BU location code. The 16 codes do not necessarily identify 16 distinct physical venues. The registry repeats venue labels for Boston codes 1090/1112 and Los Angeles codes 1023/1120. Keep these codes separate when comparing votes; confirm physical venue identities before mapping.
  • One reported section may combine several constituent electoral sections. The section CSV preserves the original DS_SECOES_AGREGADAS field.
  • Turnout equals attendance divided by registered electors in the BU. Attendance includes valid votes, blanks and nulls. Every centre’s results reconcile with the saved official municipal disclosure totals.

Sources: TSE overseas second-round ballot reports, the saved TSE 2026 round-2 polling registry, and TRE-DF’s consular jurisdiction reference (pages 10–11).
