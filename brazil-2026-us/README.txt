U.S. polling results | Brazil presidential runoff 2026

Scope: Brazil's 25 October 2026 presidential runoff, election 6258, office 1, saved 26 October snapshot.

U.S. selection: the eleven consular reporting centres listed under United States in TRE-DF's September 2026 jurisdiction reference (pages 10-11, PDF indices 9-10).

This is a U.S. consular-centre extract. Jurisdictions can include territories outside the mainland; New York's jurisdiction also includes Bermuda. It is not a voter-residence classification.

Polling-location unit: municipality code + zone + BU location code. Sixteen codes need not represent sixteen distinct physical venues.

Map and default table combine all observed sections and location codes within each of the eleven consular reporting cities. Shares are calculated from summed votes, not averaged section percentages.

Map markers use approximate city-centre coordinates and a Natural Earth public-domain contiguous-U.S. outline. They represent consular reporting totals, not physical polling-site positions or geographic voting areas.

Sections are observed BU reporting sections; one BU can aggregate constituent electoral sections. Preserve DS_SECOES_AGREGADAS for this reason.

Candidate percentages use valid votes only. Turnout uses BU registered electors; blanks and nulls are included in attendance.

Location names/addresses are from the saved TSE round-2 registry. All 358 section keys and location codes match the BU panel. Registry names are not independently verified.

The registry repeats the same venue label for Boston codes 1090/1112 and Los Angeles codes 1023/1120; these codes remain separate.

Every centre's two candidate counts, valid/blank/null counts, electorate, attendance, abstention and reporting-section count reconcile with the saved official municipal disclosure snapshot.
